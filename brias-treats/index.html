Our story

A neighbourhood oven

Bria's Treats started with one very good cinnamon bun recipe and a very small kitchen. Word travelled fast — now the lower level of the Medical Arts Building smells like brown sugar and cinnamon every weekday morning.

Everything is baked in small batches through the morning, the old-fashioned way. When the trays are empty, that's the day — so regulars know to come early.
